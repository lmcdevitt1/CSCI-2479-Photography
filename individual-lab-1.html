Origin

I was born in Columbus, Ohio and raised in the suburbs. I have lived in Columbus for all of my life.
I have two older sisters, two moms, two cats, and one dog. My parents split when I was very young,
however, they live across the street from each other, because they still care about me, and my sisters.
When I was born, I was diagnosed with a congenital heart defect. My blood would start at my heart, and go to my lungs,
then go back to my heart, and then go back to my lungs, in that same pattern. I would eventually turn blue, causing the doctors
to rush me to Nationwide Children's Hospital to diagnose me with Total Anomalous Pulmonary Venous Return (TAPVR).
